Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers regarding escrow safety, handover, and refunds

Q1 Is my money secured? (क्या पैसे सुरक्षित हैं?)

पैसा एक न्यूट्रल थर्ड पार्टी सर्विस, जिसे एस्क्रो (Escrow) कहते हैं, के पास सुरक्षित रहता है। जब खरीदार को अकाउंट मिल जाता है और वह पुष्टि कर देता है, तब पैसा सेलर को दिया जाता है।

Your payment stays 100% protected in the YT Market Escrow Vault. Funds are only transferred to the seller after your satisfactory inspection and delivery confirmation.

Q2 How does account verification work? (अकाउंट वेरिफिकेशन कैसे होता है?)

Our verification team conducts a strict 12-point audit before listing: verifying active monetization, real organic subscribers, zero active strikes, and clean ownership. Furthermore, buyers get a 24-48h live inspection window.

Q3 How do I get a refund if account isn't received or faulty?

If credentials are wrong, suspended, or not as advertised, simply click "Raise Dispute" or cancel. Escrow funds freeze instantly, and upon quick admin check, a 100% full refund is credited to your wallet or bank account.

Q4 Why must 2-Step Verification (2FA) be OFF before handover?

Logic: When the buyer logs in from a new/unrecognized device, login will not be blocked by phone OTPs, Google Prompts, or hardware keys. Once in control, the buyer immediately turns ON their own 2FA.

Q5 What is the 6-Month to 1-Year 100% Protection Guarantee?

If any previous owner claim, unauthorized recovery attempt, or backend fault occurs within 6 months to 1 year of purchase, YT Market guarantees a 100% equal channel replacement or a complete 100% cash refund.

Step-by-Step Purchasing Tips

How to ensure safety when buying an account and updating recovery credentials

1

Login & Change Password First

Upon receiving credentials, log in on a clean browser profile and immediately change the main password to a new strong passphrase.

2

Replace Seller's Recovery Data

Open Google Account Security settings. Completely delete the seller's recovery email and phone number, and bind your own personal recovery contacts.

3

Sign Out Previous Devices

Navigate to "Manage Devices" and click "Sign Out" on all previously recognized sessions/devices used by the previous owner.

4

Turn ON Your Own 2FA

Activate your personal 2-Step Verification with Google Authenticator or SMS prompt, and download your 8-digit backup codes.
